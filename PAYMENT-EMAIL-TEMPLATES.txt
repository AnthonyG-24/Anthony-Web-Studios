PAYMENT & CONTRACT EMAIL TEMPLATES
===================================

These simple email templates provide legal protection without needing a formal contract.
Save client replies as proof they agreed to terms.


===========================================
OPTION 1: STRIPE INVOICE MESSAGE (EASIEST)
===========================================

Add this to your Stripe invoice description/email:

---
By submitting payment, you agree to the refund policy at
https://[yourwebsite.com]/#pricing - deposit is refundable
until you approve the initial design mockup in writing, request
revisions, or 14 days pass.
---


=======================================================
OPTION 2: PRE-INVOICE CONFIRMATION EMAIL (RECOMMENDED)
=======================================================

Subject: Quick Confirmation Before We Start - [Client Name] Website

---
Hi [Client Name],

Excited to build your website! Before I send the invoice, let's confirm the details:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- [4/5/6/etc] page website
- Total Cost: $[amount]
- 50% Deposit: $[amount] - Fully refundable until you approve the initial design mockup in writing, request revisions, or 14 days pass
- 50% Final Payment: $[amount] - Due when your website is complete and ready to launch

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- Custom responsive design
- Contact form
- Mobile-friendly (works on all devices)
- Domain connection help
- 30 days of free post-launch support (tweaks, text, images, minor fixes)

TIMELINE:
- [2-4 weeks / 4-6 weeks] from deposit to launch
- Regular updates throughout the project

REFUND POLICY:
Your deposit is fully refundable until you approve the initial design mockup in writing (email/text). It becomes non-refundable after you approve the design, request revisions, or if 14 days pass without a response.

Please reply "CONFIRMED" or "Looks good!" to acknowledge these terms, and I'll send you the secure Stripe payment link to get started.

Full terms are also available at: https://[yourwebsite.com]/#pricing

Looking forward to working with you!

Best,
Anthony
Anthony Web Studio LLC
---

IMPORTANT: Save their "CONFIRMED" reply as proof they agreed to terms.

========================================
QUICK REFERENCE: WHAT YOU NEED TO SAVE
========================================

For legal protection, keep records of:
✓ Client's "confirmed" email reply (before sending invoice)
✓ Stripe invoice receipt (automatic)
✓ Stripe payment confirmation (automatic)
✓ Email where you send initial design mockup
✓ Client's written approval of design (email/text)
✓ Any revision requests (email/text)

These prove:
- They agreed to terms before paying
- When design approval happened (when deposit became non-refundable)
- Payment was processed


====================================
CANCELLATION REQUEST EMAIL TEMPLATE
====================================

If a client wants to cancel, use this:

Subject: Re: Cancellation Request - [Client Name]

---
Hi [Client Name],

I understand you'd like to cancel the project. Let me check the status:

[IF BEFORE DESIGN APPROVAL:]
Since we haven't reached the design approval milestone yet, your deposit is fully refundable. I'll process the refund within 2-3 business days. You should see it back in your account within 5-7 business days depending on your bank.

[IF AFTER DESIGN APPROVAL:]
Since you approved the initial design on [date] / requested revisions on [date], the deposit has become non-refundable per our agreement. However, I'll send you all work completed so far including [list what you've done].

Let me know if you have any questions.

Best,
Anthony
---


===================
TIPS FOR PROTECTION
===================

1. ALWAYS get written confirmation before sending invoice
2. ALWAYS document when you send the initial design mockup
3. ALWAYS save client's written design approval (email/text/message)
4. ALWAYS save any revision requests as proof of engagement
5. NEVER start work without deposit received
6. NEVER deliver final files without final payment

Keep all emails organized in folders by client name for easy reference.
