HOW STRIPE INVOICES WORK
-------------------------

1. You create an invoice in Stripe Dashboard with the exact amount
2. Stripe sends the client a professional email with the invoice
3. Client clicks "Pay Invoice" button in the email
4. They enter their card info and pay
5. Stripe automatically sends them a receipt
6. You get notified of the payment
7. Money goes into your Stripe account (then transfers to your bank)


================================================================================
STEP-BY-STEP: CREATING AN INVOICE
================================================================================

STEP 1: Log into Stripe Dashboard
----------------------------------
Go to: https://dashboard.stripe.com
Log in with your credentials


STEP 2: Navigate to Invoices
-----------------------------
- Click "Invoices" in the left sidebar
- Click the blue "+ New" button (top right)


STEP 3: Add Customer Information
---------------------------------
- Click "Add customer"
- Enter their email address (REQUIRED)
- Optionally add: Name, Phone number
- Click "Add customer"


STEP 4: Add Line Items (What They're Paying For)
-------------------------------------------------
Click "Add line item" and fill out:

For DEPOSIT:
- Description: "Website Development - [X] pages - Deposit (50%)"
- Quantity: 1
- Unit price: [enter the deposit amount]

Example for 5-page site:
- Description: "Website Development - 5 pages - Deposit (50%)"
- Quantity: 1
- Unit price: $525


For FINAL PAYMENT:
- Description: "Website Development - [X] pages - Final Payment (50%)"
- Quantity: 1
- Unit price: [enter the final amount]


STEP 5: Add Optional Details (Recommended)
-------------------------------------------
- Memo (optional): Add note like "Fully refundable until design approval"
- Due date: Set when you want them to pay (default is "Due on receipt")


STEP 6: Send the Invoice
-------------------------
- Click "Review invoice" (top right)
- Double-check everything is correct
- Click "Send invoice"
- Stripe immediately emails the invoice to the client

================================================================================
WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU SEND AN INVOICE
================================================================================

CLIENT RECEIVES EMAIL FROM STRIPE:
- Professional email with your business name
- Shows invoice details (description, amount)
- Big "Pay Invoice" button

WHEN THEY PAY:
- They click "Pay Invoice"
- Enter card information
- Stripe processes payment
- Client gets instant receipt via email

YOU GET NOTIFIED:
- Stripe sends you email: "Payment received"
- Invoice status changes to "Paid" in your dashboard
- Money appears in your Stripe balance

MONEY TRANSFER TO YOUR BANK:
- Stripe automatically transfers to your bank account
- Usually takes 2-3 business days
- You can see pending transfers in Dashboard → Payouts


================================================================================
CHECKING INVOICE STATUS
================================================================================

To see if someone paid:

1. Go to Stripe Dashboard → Invoices
2. Look at the "Status" column:
   - DRAFT = Not sent yet
   - OPEN = Sent but not paid yet
   - PAID = They paid! ✓
   - VOID = Cancelled

3. Click on any invoice to see full details


================================================================================
IF CLIENT HASN'T PAID YET
================================================================================

STRIPE SENDS AUTOMATIC REMINDERS:
- Stripe can automatically send payment reminders
- You can enable this in Settings

MANUAL REMINDER:
1. Go to Dashboard → Invoices
2. Find the unpaid invoice
3. Click the three dots (...)
4. Click "Send invoice" to resend


================================================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Does Stripe charge me fees?
A: Yes - 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
   Example: $400 payment = $11.90 fee (you receive $388.10)

Q: Do I need to send receipts manually?
A: NO! Stripe automatically sends receipts after every payment

Q: Can I edit an invoice after sending?
A: Not directly. You have to "void" it and create a new one.

Q: What if client says they didn't receive the invoice?
A: Check their spam folder, or resend from Stripe Dashboard

Q: Can clients save their card for the final payment?
A: Yes! Stripe can save their card info securely (you can enable this)

Q: What email address do invoices come from?
A: Stripe's email (receipts@stripe.com) but it shows YOUR business name

WHAT IF CLIENT CHANGES NUMBER OF PAGES AFTER PAYING DEPOSIT?
================================================================================

This happens! Don't panic - it's easy to handle.

THE RULE:
---------
Take the NEW total price, subtract what they already paid, and that's what
they owe you at the end.

Formula: NEW TOTAL - DEPOSIT PAID = FINAL PAYMENT


SCENARIO 1: CLIENT WANTS FEWER PAGES
-------------------------------------

Original: 5 pages ($1,050 total, deposit $525 paid ✓)
Change: "Actually, I only need 4 pages"
New total: $800

THE MATH:
- New total: $800
- Already paid: $525
- Final payment: $800 - $525 = $275

WHAT YOU DO:
1. Email them:
   "Hi [Client],

   No problem changing from 5 pages to 4 pages!

   Your new project total is $800 (instead of $1,050).

   You already paid $525 as your deposit, so your final payment when
   the site is complete will be $275 (instead of $525).

   Everything else stays the same!

   Anthony"

2. When project is done, create final invoice for $275 (not $525)
3. They pay $275
4. Total paid: $525 + $275 = $800 ✓

NOTE: You don't need to refund anything. Just adjust the final payment.


SCENARIO 2: CLIENT WANTS MORE PAGES
------------------------------------

Original: 5 pages ($1,050 total, deposit $525 paid ✓)
Change: "Actually, I want 6 pages"
New total: $1,300

THE MATH:
- New total: $1,300
- Already paid: $525
- Final payment: $1,300 - $525 = $775

WHAT YOU DO:
1. Email them:
   "Hi [Client],

   Adding the extra page will bring your total to $1,300 (instead of $1,050).

   You already paid $525 as your deposit, so your final payment when
   the site is complete will be $775.

   Sound good?

   Anthony"

2. When project is done, create final invoice for $775 (not $525)
3. They pay $775
4. Total paid: $525 + $775 = $1,300 ✓


QUICK REFERENCE - FINAL PAYMENT CALCULATOR
-------------------------------------------

If they originally wanted 5 pages (paid $525 deposit) but changed to:

3 pages ($800 total):
  Final payment = $800 - $525 = $275

4 pages ($800 total):
  Final payment = $800 - $525 = $275

5 pages ($1,050 total) - ORIGINAL PLAN:
  Final payment = $1,050 - $525 = $525

6 pages ($1,300 total):
  Final payment = $1,300 - $525 = $775

7 pages ($1,550 total):
  Final payment = $1,550 - $525 = $1,025

8 pages ($1,800 total):
  Final payment = $1,800 - $525 = $1,275


IMPORTANT TIPS FOR SCOPE CHANGES:
----------------------------------

✓ ALWAYS communicate the change in writing (email)
✓ State the NEW total clearly
✓ Show them the math so there's no confusion
✓ Get their confirmation before continuing work
✓ Update your project notes/contract with the new scope
✓ Create the final invoice for the ADJUSTED amount (not the original 50%)

CARE PLAN SUBSCRIPTION (MONTHLY $50)
================================================================================

The Care Plan is DIFFERENT from invoices. It's a recurring subscription.

HOW TO SET IT UP (ONE TIME):
-----------------------------
1. Go to Stripe Dashboard > Products
2. Click "+ Add Product"
3. Name: "Website Care Plan"
4. Description: "Monthly website maintenance and support. Includes hosting
   management, security monitoring, monthly backups, up to 2 small updates
   per month (text/image changes), and priority support. Cancel anytime
   with 30 days notice."
5. Price: $50
6. Select: RECURRING > Monthly
7. Save

CREATE A PAYMENT LINK:
-----------------------
1. Go to Stripe Dashboard > Payment Links
2. Click "+ New"
3. Select your "Website Care Plan" product
4. Customize the page if you want
5. Click "Create Link"
6. SAVE THIS LINK - you'll send it to clients

WHEN CLIENT SUBSCRIBES:
------------------------
- They click your link
- Enter their card info
- Stripe charges them $50 immediately
- Stripe auto-charges $50 every month after that
- You get paid automatically, no chasing invoices
