COMMON CLIENT QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
==================================

Q: "How does your pricing work?"
A: Early client discount: Projects start at $950 for 4 pages (regular $1,500). Each additional page beyond 4 is $400 (regular $500). For example: 5 pages = $1,350, 6 pages = $1,750, 8 pages = $2,550. You only pay for what you need. This discount is limited to the first 5 clients (4 spots left!).

Q: "What's included in free support?"
A: Text changes, image swaps, bug fixes, and basic technical help. These stay free forever. Adding new pages ($400 early / $500 regular) or custom features ($150+) cost extra even during the free support period.

Q: "Do you build online stores?"
A: I don't specialize in e-commerce right now. I focus on informational websites that showcase businesses and services.

Q: "How long does a project take?"
A: Smaller sites (4-5 pages): 4-6 weeks. Larger sites (6-8 pages): 6-8 weeks from deposit to launch.

Q: "What if I need changes after launch?"
A: Small updates (text, images) remain free forever. New pages ($400 early / $500 regular) or custom features ($150+) require additional payment.

Q: "Can you help with domain setup?"
A: Yes, I provide guidance and can help configure DNS settings, but you purchase and own the domain.
