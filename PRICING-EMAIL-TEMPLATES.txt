QUICK REFERENCE: WHEN TO USE WHICH TEMPLATE
============================================

TEMPLATE 1 (Nonprofit): Church/nonprofit inquiries, automatic 15% discount
TEMPLATE 2 (Payment Plan): Client mentions "tight budget" or asks about financing
TEMPLATE 3 (Discount Request): Client directly asks "can you do it cheaper?"
TEMPLATE 4 (Phased): Large project but limited budget now, money coming later
TEMPLATE 5 (Competing Quote): Client says "I got quoted $X elsewhere"
TEMPLATE 6 (Full Quote): Standard detailed quote for serious prospects


TIPS FOR PRICING CONVERSATIONS
===============================

1. ALWAYS respond within 24 hours to pricing inquiries
2. ASK QUESTIONS before giving a number:
   - How many pages do you need?
   - Are you a nonprofit/church?
   - What's your timeline?
   - What's your budget range?

3. PRESENT OPTIONS, not just one price:
   - Give 2-3 payment plan options
   - Offer phased approach if budget is tight
   - Show reduced scope alternative

4. BE CONFIDENT in your pricing:
   - Don't apologize for your rates
   - Explain the value you provide
   - Walk away from clients who don't value your work

5. FOLLOW UP if they don't respond:
   - Wait 3-4 days
   - Send friendly "just checking in" email
   - Offer to answer any questions
   - Don't chase more than once
