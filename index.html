Common questions about my web development process and how I'll work with you

Simple 3-step process:

  1. You purchase your domain - I'll recommend Porkbun (an affordable domain registrar where you buy your website address) and guide you through the process if needed
  2. Share temporary access - Give me your login details so I can configure everything (I recommend changing your password after setup is complete for security)
  3. I handle the technical setup - DNS and nameservers (the settings that connect your domain to your website) so everything works properly

You own the domain completely, I just handle the technical connection to make your website live!

After that, the deposit becomes non-refundable.

Terms & Policies

Last Updated: January 19, 2026

How Payments Work

Risk-free payment structure: You pay 50% upfront to start your project (fully refundable until you approve the initial design mockup in writing, request revisions, or 14 days pass), then 50% when your website is complete and ready to launch. All payments are processed securely through Stripe, and you'll get email receipts and written confirmation of these terms before starting.

Pricing: Starter Package (First 10 Clients Only): $500 for a complete website (up to 5 pages/sections). Need more? We can discuss! Standard Packages: Regular rate is $1,000 for 4 pages. Early client discount: $800 for 4 pages (limited time - save $200!). Each additional page beyond 4 is $300 during the early client discount ($350 regular rate). Payment is split 50/50: half upfront to start, half when you're happy with the final result before launch.

Refund Policy (Simple & Fair)

Deposit (first 50%): Fully refundable until you approve the initial design preview (your first look at the website design) in writing (email, text, or other written confirmation). Once you approve the design and I begin building your site, the deposit becomes non-refundable because it covers the development work I have invested. If I do not hear from you within 14 days of sending the initial design, I will send a reminder. After that reminder, the deposit becomes non-refundable.

Final Payment (last 50%): If I don't deliver what we agreed on in your package, you can request a full refund within 7 days of completion. For example, if you paid for 4 pages but only got 2, or if a promised contact form is missing.

Project Timeline & Your Role

How long it takes: Smaller sites (4-5 pages) = 2-4 weeks. Larger sites (6-8 pages) = 4-6 weeks. Timeline starts when I receive your deposit.

What I need from you: Text content, images, your logo, and any specific requirements. I'll ask for these within 3 business days of starting. If you take longer to provide materials, it may extend the timeline.

Feedback windows: I'll show you progress and ask for feedback. You have 5 business days to respond, or I'll continue with what's been approved.

What's Included vs What Costs Extra

Always free: Small updates like text changes, image swaps, bug fixes, and basic technical help. I want your website to stay current and work perfectly.

Costs extra: New pages are $300 per page during the early client discount ($350 regular rate) and include all standard content and features like text, images, FAQs, galleries, contact forms, and testimonials. Major design overhauls are also quoted based on scope.

Changes & Additions

During development: Small tweaks and revisions are included at no extra cost. Major changes (like adding extra pages beyond your package or requesting completely new features not originally discussed) would involve a separate quote and may extend your timeline.

After approval: Once you approve a design or section and I have completed that work, requesting significant changes to that approved work would involve an additional fee since it means redoing completed work.

Website Ownership & Access

You own your website: After final payment, your website is 100% yours with no ongoing fees to me. You have full control to update content, manage hosting, and make changes.

Domain ownership: You purchase and own your domain (your website address like yoursite.com). I just help connect it to your website.

Protection & Responsibilities

My responsibility: Deliver a professional website that matches your package specifications and works properly.

Your responsibility: Provide content and feedback on time. Ensure any content you give me (text, images) is legal to use and doesn't violate copyrights.

Limitations: I am not responsible for issues outside my control (like your hosting provider going down). If something does go wrong on my end, the most you would get back is what you paid for the project.

Cancellation Policy

If you cancel: You can cancel anytime before approving the initial design preview in writing for a full refund (minus payment processing fees, if applicable). Just send me an email to cancel. After you approve the design and I start building, or if I send you a reminder after 14 days and still do not hear back, the deposit becomes non-refundable. You will still receive any completed work files.

If I cancel: In the rare case I need to cancel, you'll get a full refund of all payments and any work completed for free.
