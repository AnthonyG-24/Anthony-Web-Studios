Terms & Policies

Last Updated: November 26, 2025

How Payments Work

Risk-free payment structure: You pay 50% upfront to start your project (fully refundable until you approve the initial design), then 50% when your website is complete and ready to launch. All payments are processed securely through Stripe, and you'll get email receipts for everything.

Pricing: Regular rate is $2,000 for 4 pages. Portfolio special: $950 for 4 pages (limited to first 5 clients - save $1,050!). Each additional page beyond 4 is $400 during the portfolio special ($500 regular rate). Payment is split 50/50: half upfront to start, half when you're happy with the final result before launch.

Refund Policy (Simple & Fair)

Deposit (first 50%): Fully refundable until you approve the initial design. Once you approve the design and I continue development, the deposit becomes non-refundable as it covers my invested time and work.

Final Payment (last 50%): If I don't deliver what we agreed on in your package, you can request a full refund within 7 days of completion. For example, if you paid for 4 pages but only got 2, or if a promised contact form is missing.

Project Timeline & Your Role

How long it takes: Smaller sites (4-5 pages) = 4-6 weeks. Larger sites (6-8 pages) = 6-8 weeks. Timeline starts when I receive your deposit.

What I need from you: Text content, images, your logo, and any specific requirements. I'll ask for these within 3 business days of starting. If you take longer to provide materials, it may extend the timeline.

Feedback windows: I'll show you progress and ask for feedback. You have 5 business days to respond, or I'll continue with what's been approved.

What's Included vs What Costs Extra

Always free: Small updates like text changes, image swaps, bug fixes, and basic technical help. I want your website to stay current and work perfectly.

Costs extra: New pages are $400 per page during the portfolio special ($500 regular rate) and include all standard content and features like text, images, FAQs, galleries, contact forms, and testimonials. Major design overhauls are also quoted based on scope.

Changes & Additions

During development: Small tweaks and revisions are included. Major changes (like adding extra pages beyond your package or completely new features) will require additional payment and may extend your timeline.

After approval: Once you approve a design or section, significant changes to that approved work may incur additional fees since it requires redoing completed work.

Website Ownership & Access

You own your website: After final payment, your website is 100% yours with no ongoing fees to me. You have full control to update content, manage hosting, and make changes.

Domain ownership: You purchase and own your domain (like yoursite.com). I just help connect it to your website.

Protection & Responsibilities

My responsibility: Deliver a professional website that matches your package specifications and works properly.

Your responsibility: Provide content and feedback on time. Ensure any content you give me (text, images) is legal to use and doesn't violate copyrights.

Limitations: I'm not responsible for issues beyond my control (like your hosting going down). My total liability is limited to what you paid for the project.

Cancellation Policy

If you cancel: You can cancel anytime before approving the initial design for a full refund. After design approval, the deposit becomes non-refundable, but you'll receive any completed work.

If I cancel: In the rare case I need to cancel, you'll get a full refund and any work completed for free.
