COMMON CLIENT QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
==================================

Q: "How does your pricing work?"
A: Early client discount: Projects start at $800 for 4 pages (regular $1,000). Each additional page beyond 4 is $300 (regular $350). For example: 5 pages = $1,100, 6 pages = $1,400, 8 pages = $2,000. You only pay for what you need. This discount is available for a limited time.

Q: "What's included after launch?"
A: You get a 30-day support window for small tweaks, text changes, and adjustments. After 30 days, updates are handled through the Care Plan ($50/month) or billed at $50/hour. Bug fixes for my original work are always covered.

Q: "Do you build online stores?"
A: I don't specialize in e-commerce right now. I focus on informational websites that showcase businesses and services.

Q: "How long does a project take?"
A: Smaller sites (4-5 pages): 2-4 weeks. Larger sites (6-8 pages): 4-6 weeks from deposit to launch.

Q: "What if I need changes after the 30-day window?"
A: Two options: Website Care Plan ($50/month) which includes 2 updates per month, or hourly billing at $50/hour (30-minute minimum). New pages are $300 each (early discount).

Q: "Do you offer ongoing maintenance?"
A: Yes! The Website Care Plan is $50/month. Includes hosting management, security monitoring, monthly backups, up to 2 small updates per month, and priority support. After your 30-day support window, this is the most affordable way to keep your site updated.

Q: "Can you help with domain setup?"
A: Yes, I provide guidance and can help configure DNS settings, but you purchase and own the domain.
