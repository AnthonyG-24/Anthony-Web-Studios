  *** IMPORTANT NOTE FOR ANTHONY ***

  An email reply saying "I agree" is legally binding in most US states

  DocuSign is free for up to 3 documents per month. The client receives

  stronger protection if a dispute ever comes up.

  Bottom line:
    - Under $500  -->  email reply "I agree" is fine
    - $800+       -->  use DocuSign (free at docusign.com)
