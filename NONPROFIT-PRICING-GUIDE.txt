WHEN TO OFFER DISCOUNTS
========================

✅ DO OFFER DISCOUNTS FOR:
--------------------------
1. Churches and 501(c)(3) nonprofits (automatic 15% off)
2. Referrals from existing happy clients (10% off)
3. Multiple projects booked upfront ("I need a site + my friend needs one too")
4. Trade/barter if they have skills you need (graphic design, accounting, etc.)
5. Veterans/military nonprofits (15-20% off as thank you for service)

❌ DON'T DISCOUNT FOR:
---------------------
1. "I can get it cheaper on Fiverr" - Let them go, they don't value quality
2. "I don't have a budget" with no nonprofit status - Offer payment plan instead
3. Vague promises of "future work" or "exposure" - Doesn't pay bills
4. Price shoppers who just want the cheapest option - Bad clients
5. Anyone being rude or demanding - You don't need that stress

==================================

1. Your base pricing is already very fair ($950 for 4 pages)

always want discounts no matter what you charge.
